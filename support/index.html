Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an Apple Music subscription?

Apple Music streaming requires an active subscription. Local music playback does not.

What is Meelo Pro?

Meelo Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks additional features permanently. No recurring subscription fees.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the Pro purchase page in Meelo and tap Restore Purchases.

Why are some lyrics unavailable?

Lyrics depend on availability from supported sources. Not every song has available lyrics.

How does Smart Autoplay work?

When available, Smart Autoplay helps keep the music playing with related songs. Availability may depend on the music source.

常见问题

使用 Meelo 需要订阅 Apple Music 吗？

Apple Music 在线播放需要有效订阅。 本地音乐播放不需要 Apple Music 订阅。

Meelo Pro 是订阅服务吗？

不是。Meelo Pro 采用一次性买断模式， 购买后永久解锁对应功能，不会自动续费。

如何恢复购买？

打开 Meelo 的 Pro 购买页面， 点击「恢复购买」。

为什么部分歌曲没有歌词？

歌词是否可用取决于支持的歌词来源， 并非所有歌曲都有可用歌词。

什么是智能续播？

智能续播可以在支持的音乐来源中， 自动继续播放相关歌曲。
