Meelo 隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026 年 10 月 29 日

本隐私政策说明 Meelo 在提供音乐播放、歌词显示、智能续播、 本地音乐管理及分享等功能时，如何处理相关信息。 不同功能处理的信息有所不同，具体取决于您使用的功能、 连接的音乐服务及授予的系统权限。

1. Apple Music 与音乐资料库

在您授权后，Meelo 使用 Apple 提供的 MusicKit 及相关音乐接口， 搜索和获取音乐内容、访问获授权的音乐资料库、播放歌曲， 并执行您使用相关功能时需要的收藏或歌单操作。 相关请求可能涉及搜索词、歌曲、歌手、专辑或歌单标识， 以及音乐服务地区和语言信息。 服务鉴权及相关处理由 Apple 的框架和服务完成。

Meelo 会在设备上保存部分歌曲与资料库信息、播放队列、 最近播放记录和播放进度，用于显示内容和恢复播放。 应用内断开 Apple Music 连接不等于删除全部本地记录， 也不等于撤销系统授权。您可在系统设置中的相关权限页面管理授权。

2. 歌词服务

为查找和显示歌词，Meelo 会通过 HTTPS 向 LRCLIB （lrclib.net）及网易云音乐 （music.163.com、interface3.music.163.com）发送歌曲名和歌手名。 查询网易云歌词时，还会发送搜索结果中匹配歌曲的标识。 专辑名和时长可用于设备上的匹配判断。 这些歌词请求不上传音频文件。

自动选择来源时，设备地区为中国大陆的情况下优先尝试网易云， 其他地区优先尝试 LRCLIB。选择某一歌词来源后， 应用仍可能尝试另一来源作为备选； 播放器准备可选歌词来源时，也可能查询两个服务。 歌词查询可在播放、显示队列或准备分享内容时自动发生。

Meelo 会在设备上缓存歌词、相关歌曲信息、来源偏好和查询状态， 以减少重复请求。隐藏歌词只改变显示状态，不会删除已缓存歌词， 也不是关闭全部歌词网络请求的开关。

服务提供方会接收处理网络请求所需的信息，例如来源 IP 地址、 请求参数及请求头。这些请求使用系统网络组件， 并非专门配置为无 Cookie 的匿名会话。 第三方对请求信息的处理适用其自身的政策和规则。

3. 智能续播

在 Apple Music 音源的顺序播放模式下， Meelo 可使用队列中的歌曲作为依据，通过 MusicKit 获取相关歌手、相似歌手的热门歌曲和同流派榜单， 并在设备上去重和整理后加入播放队列。 必要时，应用会尝试使用 Apple Music 提供的歌曲电台继续播放。

这些功能会向 Apple 的服务请求相应的歌曲、歌手、流派或电台资源， 并涉及相关资源标识和分页参数。 本地导入歌曲不使用这一 Apple Music 智能续播流程。

4. 可选的网易云每日推荐

如果您连接网易云每日推荐功能，Meelo 会通过扫码登录取得登录状态， 并处理网易云账号标识、昵称及登录 Cookie。 这些信息保存在设备的 Keychain 中； 相关请求会将登录 Cookie 和必要的请求校验信息发送给网易云， 以获取账号资料和每日推荐歌曲。

Meelo 会将推荐歌曲信息用于 Apple Music 搜索和匹配， 并在获得 Apple Music 授权的情况下， 创建或更新您的 Apple Music“网易云每日推荐”歌单。 在系统允许后台刷新时，相关同步也可能在后台执行。

本地推荐归档按最近七个推荐日维护， 在应用执行相关初始化或同步流程时裁剪较早记录。 这不表示全部相关缓存、匹配结果和歌单同步记录都会在七天后删除。

在 Meelo 内退出网易云登录时，应用会删除所保存的登录凭据， 尝试清除本地推荐归档和匹配缓存，并停止相关后台同步任务。 已创建的 Apple Music 歌单不会因此删除； 为识别和继续管理该歌单，本地同步记录也会保留， 其中可能包含来源账号标识、歌单标识和已同步歌曲信息。 退出连接不等于注销您的网易云账号。

5. 本地音乐、封面与试听

您导入的音频会复制或转换后保存在 Meelo 的本地存储空间中。 应用会读取歌曲名、歌手、专辑、内嵌封面及音频规格等信息， 用于管理和播放。歌词、封面匹配和试听查询不会上传导入的音频文件。

本地音频保存在设备上，不代表其歌曲信息完全不参与网络查询。 为补充封面和歌手图片，Meelo 可自动向 MusicBrainz、 TheAudioDB、Cover Art Archive、Wikidata 和 Wikimedia Commons 查询歌手名、专辑名或相关资源标识，并下载匹配的图片。

为获取动态专辑封面，Meelo 会向 artwork.m8tec.top 发送 Apple Music 专辑页面地址，或歌手名和专辑名。 该域名是第三方封面接口，不应视为 Apple 官方服务。 应用还会访问返回的媒体资源地址，以显示或缓存封面。

在使用需要试听片段的功能时，Meelo 可向 Deezer （api.deezer.com）发送歌手名和歌曲名， 并下载和缓存匹配的试听音频。 这些第三方及媒体托管方会接收提供网络服务所需的请求信息。

6. 设备上的数据存储

Meelo 使用以下本地存储方式：

  • 系统偏好存储：保存主题、显示选项、音乐来源、播放模式、 连接状态及部分搜索和播放记录。
  • 应用支持目录及缓存目录：保存歌词、音乐元数据、 播放队列、图片、动态封面、试听片段和服务同步记录。 较大的数据可能从偏好存储转存为文件。
  • 应用文档目录：保存导入的音频、内嵌封面、 本地资料库索引、歌单和收藏信息。
  • Keychain：保存网易云登录凭据。 当前实现使用仅限本设备的 Keychain 可访问性设置， 未启用这些凭据的 Keychain 同步。
  • 临时目录：保存分享导出和音频处理过程中的中间文件。

当前版本未实现通过 CloudKit 或 iCloud 容器进行的应用自有数据同步。 设备备份、从文件提供方导入内容，以及 Apple Music、 照片或其他系统服务的云端功能，是不同的处理流程， 可能受您的系统设置及相应服务规则影响。 因此，“保存在设备上”不表示相关数据绝不会包含在系统备份中。

7. 数据保留和删除

不同数据采用不同的保留方式。 设置、导入文件和部分成功查询缓存没有统一的自动删除期限。 部分缓存设有刷新或复用期限，但缓存到期不等于立即从磁盘删除。 系统也可能根据存储管理需要清理缓存或临时文件。

  • 您可在搜索界面逐项删除显示的搜索历史记录。
  • 从首页歌曲列表移除记录，不会同时清除该歌曲的全部歌词、 封面缓存或播放记录。
  • 从本地歌单移除歌曲，只改变歌单内容，不会删除已导入的音频文件。
  • 隐藏歌词不会清除歌词缓存。
  • 退出网易云连接的清理范围及保留项目，见本政策第 4 节。 断开 Apple Music 也不属于全量数据删除操作。
  • 分享或处理流程结束、取消或失败时，应用会在相应路径尝试清理临时文件； 异常退出等情况可能使部分文件暂时保留。

当前版本未提供覆盖所有存储位置的一键清除功能。 您可以通过系统的应用存储管理功能管理 Meelo， 但不应将删除应用理解为同时删除 Keychain 凭据、 系统备份、第三方账号信息、Apple Music 歌单， 或已经保存和分享至其他位置的副本。 外部服务中的内容需要在相应服务中另行管理。

8. 分享与照片权限

当您生成或分享歌词图片、视频等内容时， Meelo 会在设备上处理所选内容。 若您选择保存到照片，应用会在需要时请求相应系统权限； 若您选择通过其他应用分享，所选内容会交给您选择的应用或服务。

清理 Meelo 中的临时文件不会删除已经保存至照片、 文件或接收方服务中的副本。 分享后的内容受相应接收方的处理规则约束。

9. 分析与诊断

在分析与崩溃诊断方面，Meelo 仅使用 Apple 提供的相关能力， 不接入第三方分析或崩溃统计 SDK。 Apple 是否提供相关分析或诊断信息，取决于系统设置及 Apple 的规则。 这与前述用于歌词、封面、音乐和试听的第三方功能请求不同。

Meelo 还提供本地音乐服务与封面诊断信息， 可包含应用版本、系统版本、授权和订阅状态、 签名元数据、操作时间及错误代码。 您可以主动复制这些信息用于排查问题。 复制操作本身不会将诊断报告发送给开发者。

10. 第三方处理与政策更新

Apple 及本政策列明的第三方服务会按照各自的政策处理收到的信息。 Meelo 的本地删除操作不能删除这些服务已经持有的数据， 本政策也不替这些服务承诺统一的服务器日志保留期限。

我们可能根据功能或数据处理方式的变化更新本政策， 并在本页面调整最后更新日期。 涉及需要另行告知或授权的变化时，我们会按适用要求处理。

Meelo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Meelo handles information when providing music playback, lyrics, playback continuation, local music management, and sharing features. The information involved depends on the features you use, the music services you connect, and the system permissions you grant.

1. Apple Music and Your Music Library

With your authorization, Meelo uses Apple's MusicKit and related music interfaces to search for and retrieve music, access your authorized music library, play songs, and perform favorite or playlist operations required by the features you use. Requests may involve search terms, song, artist, album, or playlist identifiers, and music service region and language information. Authentication and related processing are handled through Apple's frameworks and services.

Meelo stores some song and library information, playback queues, recent playback records, and playback positions on your device to display content and restore playback. Disconnecting Apple Music within Meelo does not delete all local records or revoke system authorization. You can manage authorization in the relevant permission controls in system settings.

2. Lyrics Services

To find and display lyrics, Meelo sends song titles and artist names over HTTPS to LRCLIB (lrclib.net) and NetEase Cloud Music (music.163.com and interface3.music.163.com). NetEase lyrics requests also include the identifier of a matching song returned by its search service. Album names and durations may be used for matching on your device. These lyrics requests do not upload audio files.

Automatic source selection prioritizes NetEase when the device region is mainland China and LRCLIB in other regions. Selecting a particular source does not prevent Meelo from trying the other source as a fallback. Preparing the player's available lyrics sources may also involve querying both services. Lyrics requests may occur automatically during playback, while displaying queues, or when preparing content for sharing.

Meelo caches lyrics, related song information, source preferences, and lookup status on your device to reduce repeated requests. Hiding lyrics changes their display state; it does not delete cached lyrics and is not a switch that disables all lyrics requests.

Service providers receive information needed to handle network requests, such as the source IP address, request parameters, and request headers. These requests use system networking components and are not specifically configured as cookie-free anonymous sessions. Third-party handling is subject to those providers' own policies and rules.

3. Playback Continuation

In sequential playback mode with Apple Music as the music source, Meelo may use a song in the queue to request related artists, popular songs by similar artists, and genre charts through MusicKit. Meelo removes duplicates and organizes the results on your device before adding them to the queue. When necessary, it may try an Apple Music song station to continue playback.

These features request song, artist, genre, or station resources from Apple and involve relevant resource identifiers and pagination parameters. Locally imported songs do not use this Apple Music continuation process.

4. Optional NetEase Daily Recommendations

If you connect NetEase daily recommendations, Meelo obtains a sign-in session through QR-code login and handles your NetEase account identifier, nickname, and login cookies. This information is stored in your device's Keychain. Relevant requests send login cookies and necessary request verification information to NetEase to retrieve account details and daily recommended songs.

Meelo uses recommended song information to search for matching songs on Apple Music. With Apple Music authorization, it creates or updates a NetEase daily recommendations playlist in your Apple Music library. Synchronization may also run in the background when permitted by the system.

The local recommendations archive is maintained for the most recent seven recommendation days. Older entries are pruned during relevant initialization or synchronization processes. This does not mean that all related caches, matching results, and playlist synchronization records are deleted after seven days.

Signing out of NetEase within Meelo deletes the stored login credentials, attempts to remove the local recommendations archive and matching cache, and stops related background synchronization. It does not delete playlists already created in Apple Music. Local synchronization records are retained to identify and manage that playlist and may include the source account identifier, playlist identifiers, and synchronized song information. Disconnecting does not close your NetEase account.

5. Local Music, Artwork, and Previews

Imported audio is copied or converted and stored in Meelo's local storage. The app reads information such as song titles, artists, albums, embedded artwork, and audio specifications for management and playback. Lyrics, artwork matching, and preview lookups do not upload your imported audio files.

Keeping audio files on your device does not mean their metadata is never used in network requests. To supplement album artwork and artist images, Meelo may automatically query MusicBrainz, TheAudioDB, Cover Art Archive, Wikidata, and Wikimedia Commons using artist names, album names, or related resource identifiers, and download matching images.

To retrieve animated album artwork, Meelo sends an Apple Music album page URL, or an artist name and album name, to artwork.m8tec.top. This domain is a third-party artwork interface and should not be treated as an official Apple service. Meelo also accesses returned media URLs to display or cache artwork.

When you use a feature that requires a preview clip, Meelo may send an artist name and song title to Deezer (api.deezer.com) and download and cache a matching audio preview. These providers and media hosts receive the request information needed to deliver their services.

6. Storage on Your Device

Meelo uses the following local storage locations:

  • System preferences: themes, display options, music source, playback mode, connection state, and some search and playback records.
  • Application Support and cache directories: lyrics, music metadata, playback queues, images, animated artwork, preview clips, and service synchronization records. Larger data may be moved from preferences into files.
  • The app's Documents directory: imported audio, embedded artwork, the local library index, playlists, and favorites.
  • Keychain: NetEase login credentials. The current implementation uses a device-only Keychain accessibility setting and does not enable Keychain synchronization for these credentials.
  • Temporary storage: intermediate files used for sharing, exporting, and audio processing.

The current version does not implement its own app data synchronization through CloudKit or iCloud containers. Device backups, importing files from file providers, and cloud features offered by Apple Music, Photos, or other system services are separate processes governed by your settings and the relevant service rules. Local storage therefore does not mean that data can never be included in a system backup.

7. Retention and Deletion

Different data has different retention behavior. Settings, imported files, and some successful lookup caches do not have a single automatic deletion period. Some caches have refresh or reuse periods, but expiration does not mean immediate deletion from disk. The system may also remove caches or temporary files as part of storage management.

  • You can individually delete displayed search history entries in the search interface.
  • Removing a song entry from the home list does not also remove all associated lyrics, artwork caches, or playback records.
  • Removing a song from a local playlist changes that playlist without deleting the imported audio file.
  • Hiding lyrics does not clear the lyrics cache.
  • Section 4 describes what is removed and retained when you disconnect NetEase. Disconnecting Apple Music is also not a full data deletion operation.
  • The app attempts to clean up temporary files in relevant completion, cancellation, and failure paths. Unexpected termination may leave some files temporarily retained.

The current version does not offer a single control that clears every storage location. You can manage Meelo through the system's app storage controls, but deleting the app should not be understood as also deleting Keychain credentials, system backups, third-party account information, Apple Music playlists, or copies already saved or shared elsewhere. Content held by external services must be managed separately in those services.

8. Sharing and Photos Permission

When you generate or share lyrics images, videos, or other content, Meelo processes the selected content on your device. If you choose to save to Photos, the app requests the relevant system permission when needed. If you share through another app, the selected content is passed to the app or service you choose.

Removing temporary files from Meelo does not delete copies already saved to Photos, Files, or a recipient's service. Shared content is subject to the recipient's handling rules.

9. Analytics and Diagnostics

For analytics and crash diagnostics, Meelo uses only capabilities provided by Apple and does not integrate third-party analytics or crash reporting SDKs. The availability of analytics or diagnostics from Apple depends on system settings and Apple's rules. This is separate from the third-party requests used for lyrics, artwork, music, and previews described above.

Meelo also provides local music service and artwork diagnostics, which may include app and system versions, authorization and subscription status, signing metadata, operation times, and error codes. You can choose to copy this information for troubleshooting. Copying a report does not itself send it to the developer.

10. Third Parties and Policy Updates

Apple and the third-party services identified in this policy process the information they receive under their own policies. Local deletion actions in Meelo cannot delete information already held by those services. This policy does not promise a uniform server-log retention period on their behalf.

We may update this policy as features or data handling practices change and revise the last-updated date on this page. Where a change requires additional notice or authorization, we will handle it in accordance with applicable requirements.
